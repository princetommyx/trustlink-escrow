The marketplace coordination flow.

Harmonizing multi-party milestones into one trackable timeline.

1

Order Initiation

Platform mints a transaction link for a buyer order containing items from a registered marketplace vendor.

2

Buyer Deposit

Buyer deposits funds via Mobile Money USSD prompt. Platform and vendor receive status confirmation.

3

Vendor Delivery

Vendor dispatches the item to the buyer, providing courier waybills and tracking references to the platform.

4

Confirmation & Release

Buyer confirms item delivery. Release begins according to configured platform rules and fee allocation.
