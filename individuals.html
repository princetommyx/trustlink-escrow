The four-step peer-to-peer process.

Simple, transparent, and built around Ghana’s primary Mobile Money networks.

1

Terms agreed

The buyer or seller initiates a transaction specifying item description, delivery location, and agreed price in GH₵.

2

Buyer deposits funds

The buyer approves a Mobile Money USSD prompt (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or AT Money). Funds are safely ring-fenced.

3

Seller dispatches order

The seller receives confirmation that funds are secured and dispatches the item via courier, waybill, or personal pickup.

4

Buyer confirms & releases

The buyer inspects the delivered item and confirms receipt via the link, initiating payment release to the seller’s wallet.

Process transparency & rules.

Trust is built on clear rules, observable status changes, and fair handling when expectations differ.

What buyers should check

Review the exact item description, price, delivery method, and return window before approving the USSD prompt on your mobile money line.

Evidence to keep

Retain waybill receipts, courier tracking notes, chat agreements, and unboxing photos/videos in case a delivery discrepancy occurs.

When support steps in

If an order is flagged or disputed, release is held while our team reviews submitted evidence according to our standard platform rules.
