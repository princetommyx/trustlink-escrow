How TrustLink Works

Four simple steps to build trust between you and your customers on any platform.

1

Create a Link

Provide the buyer's details along with the price. Our system automatically sends the secure payment link to the buyer via SMS, Email, or WhatsApp.

2

Buyer Deposits

The buyer pays securely via Mobile Money. The payment enters a protected transaction flow that both parties can track.

3

Ship & Fulfill

Deliver the product or service with confidence. Both parties receive automated progress alerts.

4

Get Paid

Once the buyer confirms receipt, funds are released to your wallet. Clear release and dispute rules protect both sides.

Protection on both sides of the deal

TrustLink only works if the buyer and the seller can both trust it. Here's exactly what each side gets.

Buyer Protection

  • Your money stays in escrow

    Funds are held by TrustLink, not the seller, until you personally confirm you received your order.

  • Delivery confirmation, not autopay

    Sellers can't release funds themselves. Only your confirmation - via your private one-time link - moves the money.

  • A real dispute process

    Something wrong with your order? Raise a dispute and funds stay locked while our team reviews it - no money moves until it's resolved.

Seller Protection

  • Funds secured before you ship

    The buyer pays upfront and it's held in escrow. You dispatch only after seeing the payment is secured - never on a promise.

  • Direct Mobile Money payouts

    Once the buyer confirms, funds land in your TrustLink wallet and withdraw straight to MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or AT Money.

  • No surprise reversals

    Once funds are released, that's final. A dispute is the only way payment gets pulled back, and it goes through TrustLink's review first.
