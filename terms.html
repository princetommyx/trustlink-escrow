7. Creating a protected transaction

A seller creates a protected transaction by specifying item details, price in Ghana Cedis (GH₵), buyer contact number, and agreed fee split. A unique link is generated for sharing in direct messages (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, X). The buyer must review the transaction parameters before initiating payment.

8. Buyer payment responsibilities

Buyers must provide an active Ghanaian Mobile Money phone number capable of approving USSD prompt transfers. Buyers must review the item name, price, and escrow fee prior to authorizing the payment PIN on their handset. TrustLink never prompts for or stores your Mobile Money PIN.

9. Seller description and fulfilment responsibilities

10. Delivery, confirmation and release

Upon receiving the package, the buyer must inspect the contents and confirm receipt using their confirmation link or PIN. When the buyer confirms delivery, the transaction status transitions to completed and the net sale amount is credited to the seller's available wallet balance. Payment release depends on buyer confirmation or administrative dispute resolution.

11. Fees and fee allocation

TrustLink charges a transparent transaction service fee (typically 3% of item value, unless customized during link creation). The fee allocation rule (e.g., 50/50 split, buyer pays 100%, or seller pays 100%) is established when the link is created and explicitly displayed on the checkout screen prior to payment.

12. Disputes, evidence and transaction holds

If a buyer receives incorrect, damaged, or non-delivered goods, the buyer may trigger the "Raise Dispute" control prior to confirming receipt. Raising a dispute immediately freezes funds in state DISPUTED and flags the transaction for administrative arbitration. Both parties must submit proof (chat transcripts, dispatch receipts, waybills, video unboxing) to enable review.

13. Refunds and failed payment-provider transfers

If a dispute is resolved in favor of the buyer, or if an escrow order is canceled prior to shipping, funds are scheduled for refund to the buyer’s Mobile Money wallet. If a disbursement transfer fails due to payment-gateway network downtime, funds are safely re-credited to the respective account balance pending retry.
