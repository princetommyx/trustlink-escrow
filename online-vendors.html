The social vendor sales workflow.

From initial DM inquiry to instant Mobile Money withdrawal.

1

Agree in DM

Discuss the item, color, size, and price with your customer on WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, or X.

2

Generate TrustLink

Enter order details and price in GH₵ in your seller dashboard to generate a custom checkout URL.

3

Share link & buyer pays

Paste the link in the DM. The buyer reviews the details and deposits funds via Mobile Money USSD prompt.

4

Fulfil & withdraw

Ship the package via local courier. Upon buyer confirmation, funds land in your vendor wallet for instant MoMo withdrawal.
