E-commerce integration lifecycle.

How store checkouts connect to TrustLink transaction states.

1

Cart Checkout

Customer selects TrustLink Escrow at store checkout. Order details and amount in GH₵ are sent to mint a transaction.

2

MoMo Payment

Customer approves USSD Push prompt on their mobile line. Payment is verified and held safely in escrow.

3

Store Dispatch

Store operations receive payment notification and dispatches the item via courier, updating shipment status.

4

Delivery & Settlement

Customer confirms delivery receipt. Payment release is authorized to the store’s merchant wallet.
